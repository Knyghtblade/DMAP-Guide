DMAP Guide - The Production Guide to Dungeon Masters
=====================================================

GETTING THE FOLDER ONTO YOUR PC (once)
--------------------------------------
The app only works from a copy of this folder that OneDrive keeps on your PC.
Opening "DMAP Guide.exe" from the OneDrive website does NOT work: the website
hands you a lone copy of the app with no guide beside it.

1. Make sure the OneDrive app on your PC is signed in with your work account
   (the cloud icon near the clock should not say "sign in" or show an error).
2. On the OneDrive website, open "Shared", find the "DMAP Guide" folder, and
   choose "Add shortcut to My files".
3. Open File Explorer, click OneDrive on the left, and open the "DMAP Guide"
   folder that now appears. You should see "DMAP Guide.exe", a folder called
   "guide" and this READ ME side by side.
4. Right-click that folder and choose "Always keep on this device". Wait for
   the green ticks.
5. Right-click "DMAP Guide.exe", choose "Send to", then "Desktop (create
   shortcut)". From now on, open the guide from that desktop shortcut.

OPENING THE GUIDE
-----------------
Double-click the desktop shortcut (or "DMAP Guide.exe" in this folder).
Nothing needs installing.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once.

If the app opens with a page saying "The guide isn't beside the app" or "This
copy of the guide isn't shared", it was opened from a download or a copy that
OneDrive isn't syncing. Close it, delete that copy, and follow the steps above.

If "DMAP Guide.exe" is greyed out, has a cloud icon, or won't start, this folder
hasn't finished syncing to your PC yet. In OneDrive, right-click the folder and
choose "Always keep on this device", wait for the ticks, then try again.

If you can read the guide but there is no "Edit Page" button, the folder has been
shared with you as view-only. Ask the producer if you need to make changes.

SEEING EACH OTHER'S CHANGES
---------------------------
Changes other people make appear on their own while the guide is open, usually
within a minute. Press the "Refresh" button (or F5) at the top of the page to
save your changes and pick up everyone else's straight away.

To check you are on the shared guide, open Settings. "Kept in" should show a
path inside OneDrive that ends in "DMAP Guide\guide".

WHICH VERSION AM I ON?
----------------------
The version number is in the title bar of the window and at the bottom of the
Settings page, e.g. "DMAP Guide 1.0.4". Everyone should see the same number.
If a red note says a newer DMAP Guide has been used with this guide, close the
app and open "DMAP Guide.exe" from the shared folder again.
